## Short Description (50 chars)
Format SD cards to FAT32 directly on Cardputer

## Full Description

Format your SD card to FAT32 directly on your M5Stack Cardputer without needing a computer! Simple, safe, and beginner-friendly.

**Perfect for:**
- New Cardputer users setting up their SD cards
- Fixing corrupted filesystem issues
- Quick reformatting between different apps
- Creating a clean, organized folder structure

**Key Features:**
- ✅ Formats up to 128GB+ SD cards to FAT32
- ✅ No computer required - all done on-device
- ✅ Safety confirmations prevent accidental data loss
- ✅ Visual progress with step-by-step feedback
- ✅ Auto-creates organized folder structure
- ✅ Simple keyboard controls (F/G/Enter keys)
- ✅ Professional UI with color-coded status

**What You Get:**
After formatting, your SD card has a clean structure ready for apps, data, configs, screenshots, images, and sounds.

**Easy to Use:**
1. Launch from M5Launcher
2. Press F to start
3. Press F again to confirm
4. Wait 2-5 minutes
5. Done!

**Safe & Reliable:**
- Double confirmation required
- Shows real-time progress
- ESC to cancel anytime
- Verifies write capability after formatting

**Compatible with:** M5Stack Cardputer & Cardputer ADV running M5Launcher/EasyLauncher

---

**⚠️ Warning:** Formatting erases all data. Backup important files first!
